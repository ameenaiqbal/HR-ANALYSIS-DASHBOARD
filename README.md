# HR-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD
It is a  project provides insights into employee overview, salary distribution, and employee attrition. Together, these dashboards help management understand workforce strength, salary structure, employee turnover, and areas that need improvement.
# Features 
Three-page interactive HR dashboard covering Employee Overview, Salary Analysis, and Attrition Analysis.
Comprehensive KPI monitoring for tracking employee strength and attrition.
Dynamic slicers and filters for department, job title, location, employment type, and dates.
Multi-dimensional analysis using gender, age, salary bands, departments, and locations.
Geographical visualization for understanding workforce distribution.
User-friendly navigation to switch between dashboard pages.
Data-driven decision support to help HR management improve employee retention and workforce planning.
# Employee Analysis Overview

This dashboard provides an overall view of the organization's workforce.

- KPI Cards: Displays Active Employees, Employees Left, and Attrition Rate.
  
- Department Analysis: Shows employee distribution across different departments.
  
- Gender and Age Analysis: Analyzes employee distribution by gender and age group.
 
- Performance Analysis: Categorizes employees into High, Average, and Low performance levels.
 
- Geographical Analysis: Uses a map to display employee distribution across office locations.
  
- Salary Distribution: Displays employee distribution across four salary bands.
  
- Review Status Analysis: Tracks Completed, Pending, and Not Due performance reviews.
  
- Employment Type Analysis: Shows employee distribution by job type and department.

  This dashboard focuses on employee salary distribution and workforce compensation categories.

- Department-wise Salary Analysis: Compares salary band distribution across departments.
- Age Group Analysis: Analyzes salary band distribution across different age groups.
- Gender-based Analysis: Compares employee salary distribution by gender.
- Employment Type Analysis: Displays salary band distribution among Full-Time, Contract, and Part-Time employees.
- Location-based Analysis: Compares salary band distribution across different office locations.
- Interactive Date Filter: Allows users to analyze employees based on their hire dates.
- Dynamic Filtering: Provides filters for employment type, job title, and age group.

This dashboard helps management understand employee turnover and identify the areas experiencing higher attrition.
- Attrition KPI Cards: Displays Resigned and Terminated employee counts.
- Year-wise Attrition Analysis: Tracks employee exits across different years.
- Department-wise Attrition: Identifies departments with higher employee exits.
- Location-based Attrition: Compares employee exits across office locations.
- Gender-based Attrition: Analyzes employee turnover by gender.
- Age Group Analysis: Identifies which age groups experience higher employee exits.
- Salary Band Analysis: Shows employee exits across different salary bands.
- Exit Date Filter: Allows users to analyze employee attrition within a selected date range.
